SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

Data & scope.

Scope of the analysis

775 nomination records across leading acting, supporting acting and directing, with year labels spanning 1994–2024. Leading and supporting acting each combine the male and female award categories.

Nominations are grouped by race and gender into four intersectional categories: White Male, White Female, BIPOC Male and BIPOC Female. BIPOC stands for Black, Indigenous and People of Color.

Nomination share = a group’s nominations ÷ all nominations in the selected view. Win rate = a group’s wins ÷ that group’s nominations. Winners are included in nomination totals.

Year and counting conventions

Year labels include both film-release and ceremony years; some award cohorts overlap. Repeated nominations for the same person are counted separately. Joint directing nominations count as one record.
